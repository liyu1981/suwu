Suwu

Make the remote devshell enjoyable
in agentic AI time.

Your real shell, in a browser tab. Suwu gives agents and humans alike a tiling terminal that keeps running when you don't — refresh, disconnect, come back tomorrow, pick up where you left off.

Install Suwu View source

Install

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/liyu1981/suwu/refs/heads/master/install.sh | sh

The download lands in ~/.local/bin/suwu and walks you through onboarding.

See it in action.

Split panes, run real commands, refresh the page — the screen comes back.

Built for the way you actually work.

  1. 01

    Your real shell, in a browser tab

    Everything you run is running in your actual shell — not a simulation, not a sandbox. Open a tab on your laptop, tablet, or phone and it's the same machine.

    real-shell.gif
  2. 02

    Sessions that survive

    Refresh the page, drop the connection, even restart the server — reconnect and your terminal screen is replayed exactly as you left it, before live output resumes.

    session-restore.gif
  3. 03

    A workspace that moves at keyboard speed

    Split, focus, move, swap, and close tiles without touching the mouse. Group them into spaces and switch workspaces like a window manager on your desktop.

    tiling-spaces.gif
  4. 04

    More than terminals, right in the grid

    Browse and download remote files with the built-in file browser, watch logs with auto-refresh in the viewer, and forward TCP/UDP ports with a forwarding tile — all side by side with your shells.

    tile-apps.gif
  5. 05

    A CLI that talks back to you

    suwu send posts notifications to your screen from any script — pipe stdin, set a title. suwu open resolves links and actions on the remote machine. Long builds finish? You'll know.

    cli-notify.gif
  6. 06

    Reachable, but only by you

    Access works out of the box on localhost, your LAN, or over the internet behind a reverse proxy — with password authentication, per-run tokens, and one-command HTTPS.

    secure-access.gif
  7. 07

    Yours to tune

    Pick your font family and size, theme the colors, dial in a glassy background alpha. The interface speaks English and Chinese out of the box.

    appearance-i18n.gif
  8. 08

    Install once, stay current

    Onboarding sets up your dev environment step by step, and suwu update keeps the binary fresh. No config files to babysit.

    onboard-update.gif

Keys, not chrome.

Every binding is chosen to avoid clashing with common shell and readline keys.

Standing on shoulders.

Suwu is inspired by Omarchy — the opinionated, keyboard-first Arch + Hyprland setup from DHH and team. If a desktop can be joyful to drive entirely from the keyboard, so can a browser terminal.
